# Governance & Safety

## Safety stance
CliniTrace provides AI-assisted documentation and monitoring support.
It does not diagnose, prescribe, or replace clinicians.

## Human-in-the-loop
- clinician review is central
- AI outputs are assistive
- uncertainty triggers “review required”

## Data governance
- consent capture
- minimal collection
- de-identification where feasible
- retention policy aligned to clinical needs

## Model governance
- versioning
- performance monitoring
- drift detection
- rollback capability

## Ethical principles
- transparency and explainability
- fairness and bias monitoring
- accountability and auditability

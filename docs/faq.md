# FAQ

## Is CliniTrace a diagnostic tool?
No. It is designed for documentation and longitudinal monitoring support.

## Who is it for?
Clinics, home nursing providers, telehealth providers, and care teams monitoring visually-assessed conditions.

## What’s the first use case?
Wound and skin progression monitoring.

## What makes it defensible?
Doctor-led governance, quality gating, longitudinal tracking, auditability, and safety boundaries.

# CliniTrace

**Doctor-led visual triage & longitudinal monitoring** — built for clinical workflows, remote care, and consistent documentation.

## What CliniTrace is
CliniTrace is an AI-assisted platform that helps clinicians and care teams:
- Capture usable clinical images (quality coaching + gating)
- Generate **structured visual summaries**
- Track **progression over time**
- Support risk-banded escalation pathways

## What CliniTrace is not
- Not a diagnostic engine
- Not a replacement for clinicians
- Not an emergency service

## Why now
Telemedicine and remote monitoring need **standardized, defensible visual documentation**. CliniTrace focuses on consistency, workflow fit, and safety.

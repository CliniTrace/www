# Investor Proposal (Seed / Pre-Seed)

## One-liner
CliniTrace is a **doctor-led AI visual monitoring platform** that turns subjective clinical observation into **structured, measurable, time-stamped documentation** and **longitudinal progression tracking**.

## Problem
Care teams visually assess wounds/skin conditions and post-op healing daily, but:
- image quality is inconsistent,
- documentation is variable,
- escalation is delayed,
- medico-legal defensibility is weak.

## Solution
CliniTrace provides:
- capture coaching + quality gating
- measurement overlays and change detection
- progression timeline (improving / stable / deteriorating)
- structured summaries for clinical notes
- clinician review portal + audit log

## Market wedge (Phase 1)
Wound and skin monitoring in:
- primary care, home nursing, post-op follow-up, telehealth.

## Business model
- SaaS subscription per provider / per clinic
- enterprise licensing for groups
- future: API integrations + insurer programs

## Competitive advantage
- doctor-led governance & labeling rubric
- longitudinal tracking-first (not single-image novelty)
- compliance-aware, non-diagnostic positioning
- built for low-friction deployment (web/mobile)

## Milestones
- MVP: 90 days
- Pilot: 2–3 clinics, 2–4 weeks
- V1: 6–8 months (monitoring, drift metrics, export, RBAC)
- Scale: 12 months (10–20 clinics, regional rollout)

## Funding use
- product + model refinement
- clinical validation pilot
- security, audit logging, compliance advisory
- early go-to-market

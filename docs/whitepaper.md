# White Paper — CliniTrace

## Abstract
CliniTrace is a doctor-led AI-assisted visual monitoring platform designed to improve consistency, documentation quality, and longitudinal tracking in remote and in-person care workflows.

## The consistency gap
Visual assessments are clinically important but inconsistently captured and documented. Variation in lighting, distance, and angle reduces usability and increases risk.

## Approach
CliniTrace prioritizes:
- **quality-gated acquisition**
- **structured clinical outputs**
- **longitudinal change detection**
- **human-in-the-loop review**

## Safety boundaries
CliniTrace is positioned as:
- documentation + monitoring augmentation
- not diagnostic decision-making

## Implementation
- web/mobile capture
- CV overlays and trend scoring
- audit logging and access controls
- privacy-by-design storage and retention

## Evaluation
Pilot metrics:
- retake rate
- time-to-note completion
- escalation latency
- clinician confidence and usability scoring
